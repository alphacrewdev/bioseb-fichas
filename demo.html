Nosotros · Empresa familiar · Desde 2011

Todas nuestras cepas nacen de investigación ecuatoriana.

Elaboramos bioproductos basados en microorganismos para el control de plagas y enfermedades, y para fortalecer el suelo que alimenta la planta. Quince años acompañando al agro exportador.

Misión

Promover el uso de bioproductos de alta calidad, con los mejores estándares técnicos, para una agricultura más amigable con el ambiente y económicamente rentable.

Visión

Ser la empresa líder en investigación, innovación y desarrollo de productos de origen microbiano para optimizar la producción agrícola.

Nota de implementación

Sí puede ser la portada — con cuatro reglas

The objection was never the animation. It was making it a gate. This page is the same story as the standalone experience, restructured as a layer. Four things make that difference:

  • No entry screen. There is no "Entrar" button, no loader you must sit through. The H1, the value proposition and the navigation are on screen in the first frame, before the canvas has drawn anything.
  • The nav is an exit. An agronomist who came for a datasheet reaches Productos in one tap — on desktop it is a link, on a phone it is the first item in the menu. They never see chapter two, and that is a success, not a failure.
  • The story is compressed and bounded. Four chapters over 340vh on desktop, 300svh on phones. It ends, the real page slides over it, and the canvas stops rendering entirely.
  • The page is complete without it. Use Sin WebGL in the header, or the last item in the phone menu. Everything still works: headline, copy, buttons, finder, products, navigation.
La prueba real

The test for whether an animated homepage is safe is not how good the animation is. It is: delete the canvas — is this still a complete, indexable, usable homepage? If yes, be as ambitious as you like. If no, it belongs on its own route.

Concretely: the <h1> must be real text in the HTML and must never be the LCP element waiting on WebGL. Server-render the content, initialise the canvas after paint, and Core Web Vitals stay intact.

Comportamiento en móvil

Six things are handled specifically for phones. The sticky canvas is sized in svh, not vh, so it never jumps when the address bar collapses — and the resize handler ignores height-only changes for the same reason. Text panes anchor to the bottom of the screen and always read left-aligned, so the canvas stays visible above them. The nav collapses to a menu that still puts Productos one tap away. Cursor parallax is replaced by a slow autonomous drift, since there is no cursor. Tap targets grow on touch devices. And overflow-x uses clip rather than hidden, because hidden on the body silently breaks position: sticky in several mobile browsers.

Quality is adaptive: the renderer measures its own frame time and steps down through a ladder of resolution settings if the device cannot hold pace. The petri-dish scene is 2D signed-distance math rather than volumetric raymarching, so even the opening tier costs a fraction of a typical WebGL hero — and it can still degrade twice more before falling back to a static gradient.

Lo que sigue siendo cierto

The longer, slower, sound-led version still deserves to exist at /experiencia — full six chapters, voice-over, photogrammetry assets. This homepage is the trailer. That one is the film.
